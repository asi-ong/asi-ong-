Our mission

We believe that cleaning up a neighborhood and building its youth's future are part of the same work.

From Akébé-Plaine, ASI-ONG builds local, lasting solutions: household waste collection, transforming plastic into useful materials, training young leaders, community radio outreach, and health support for families.

Every program is designed to outlast the funding that launched it: that is what distinguishes a one-off intervention from real change.

ASI-ONG operates across the whole of Gabon (urban, rural, riverine, and prison settings) and also runs international initiatives, notably in Burkina Faso, where the association has been authorized to operate since 2012.

President's Message

Dear members, partners, and supporters,

As this new year begins, I address each of you with deep gratitude and renewed enthusiasm. The dawn of a new year is always a special moment: it invites us to take stock of our past actions, and above all to look ahead with boldness and determination.

Today, the challenges our world faces are immense. Social precarity, humanitarian vulnerability, and environmental emergencies remind us every day how closely our fates are linked. Nothing that touches humanity and the planet can leave us indifferent.

It is precisely at the heart of this reality that the mission of the NGO Action Sociale Internationale (ASI-ONG) takes on its full meaning.

As this new year begins, I issue a vibrant call to mobilize all our strength: to our members, the beating heart of our organization, whose energy and dedication drive our impact; to our partners, whose trust and strategic, financial, and technical support are the pillars of our collective success.

"Social and humanitarian action is not simply a response to emergency; it is a deep investment in human dignity and the preservation of our common home."

Let us commit together, starting today, to making this year a period of exceptional impact. Let us turn every one of our projects into a haven of hope for the most vulnerable and a concrete act for the safeguarding of our entire planet.

May our collective determination be the light that guides those who need it most. I wish you all a wonderful and fruitful year, marked by solidarity, hope, and action.

Alain Fabrice Vikings KOUMBA PAMBOU MVONE EFOUAPresident of the NGO Action Sociale Internationale (ASI-ONG)
Our programs

Six levers, one shared ground

Each program stands on its own but draws on the same teams and neighborhoods, building an impact that compounds over time.

01

Sanitation & pre-collection

Coordination of household waste collection routes via SYPIG/SEPI, under municipal decrees, across 11 zones of Libreville.

Partner: Ingénieurs sans Frontières / IHAP
02

Plastic circular economy

Transforming collected plastic waste into eco-friendly paving stones, a project moving toward an OAPI patent filing.

03

Radio ASI-FM

A community radio station to inform, raise awareness, and give neighborhoods a voice on sanitation and citizenship issues.

04

Les Leaders de Demain (LLDD)

A training and civic engagement platform for Gabonese youth, including international election observation missions.

05

Public health

Therapeutic education on sickle cell disease; awareness and screening for HIV/AIDS, malaria, tuberculosis, and Mpox (2024, with the Ministry of Health).

Partners: PNLP/Ministry of Health, World Bank/TIPPEE, Médecins d'Afrique/AFD, PMTCT Project, Global Fund

Road traffic counting mission on the Ndéndé–Doussala corridor

Election observation

7th international election observation mission — Benin

In April 2026, a 6-member LLDD/ASI-ONG delegation, accredited by CENA, covered 25 polling stations in Cotonou and Ouidah during Benin's presidential election.

Election observation

International election observation mission — Côte d'Ivoire

On October 25, 2025, an ASI-ONG/LLDD delegation observed the Ivorian presidential election, alongside LICAM-DH (Cameroon), ENVD, and ESA (Togo), under the banner Synergy of African Civil Society Election Experts. A joint preliminary statement was issued.
